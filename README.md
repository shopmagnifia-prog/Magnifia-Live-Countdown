# Somali eBook Growth System (Calgary)

A complete operating system to launch, sell, and scale a Somali food eBook using automation, AI (GPT-5), and a repeatable content-to-sales pipeline.

---

## 0) Executive Outcome

**Goal:** Build a self-sustaining digital business where short-form content continuously drives leads and eBook sales.

**Core promise:**
- One centralized dashboard (Notion)
- One content engine (IG/TikTok/YouTube)
- One lead funnel (free value → email/SMS capture → offer)
- One automation backbone (Zapier/Make + Klaviyo/Mailchimp)
- One revenue stack (Website + Amazon Kindle)

## 3) Content Engine (Traffic Machine)

Every content piece should map to one of three intents:
1. **Discover** (new audience)
2. **Trust** (authority + story)
3. **Convert** (lead magnet + eBook CTA)

### Instagram Plan
**Pillars**
- Somali recipes
- Book highlights
- Behind-the-scenes cooking
- Culture stories
- Quick tips

**Cadence**
- 1 Reel/day
- 1 Carousel every 2 days
- Stories daily (polls, Q&A, social proof)

### TikTok Plan
**Formats**
- 15–30 second food hooks
- “How Somali food is made” education
- Recipe reveals + ingredient hacks
- Book previews + before/after style value clips

**Cadence**
- 1–3 videos/day

### YouTube Plan
- **Shorts:** repurpose best TikTok/Reels clips
- **Long-form:** weekly cooking/culture episodes with CTA to lead magnet

### Content Production Workflow
1. Batch 10–20 hooks with GPT-5
2. Produce 1 filming day/week
3. Edit into multi-platform formats
4. Schedule + publish
5. Repurpose top performers
6. Feed insights back into next batch

## 8) GPT-5 AI System (Content + Marketing Assistant)

Use GPT-5 as an internal growth operator across copy, planning, and optimization.

### GPT-5 Use Cases
- Hook generation for reels/shorts
- Caption writing with CTAs
- Email sequence drafting
- Product page copy optimization
- A/B test variant generation
- Weekly KPI analysis + next-step recommendations

### Recommended Prompt Templates

#### 1) Hook Generator
"You are a growth strategist for a Somali food eBook brand in Calgary. Generate 30 short-form video hooks focused on curiosity, relatability, and practical cooking outcomes. Keep each hook under 12 words and include a CTA angle." 

#### 2) Caption Writer
"Write 10 Instagram captions for a Somali recipe reel. Tone: warm, practical, culturally proud. Include a 1-line story, 3 value bullets, and CTA to download free Somali recipe PDF." 

#### 3) Email Sequence Builder
"Create a 7-email nurture sequence for new leads who downloaded a free Somali recipe PDF. Goal: convert to eBook sale by email 5. Include subject lines, preview text, body copy, and CTA for each email." 

#### 4) Weekly Analyst
"Analyze the following weekly metrics (views, saves, clicks, opt-ins, sales). Identify the top 3 bottlenecks and propose a next-week action plan with priority ranking."
