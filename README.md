# Somali eBook Growth System

A complete, automation-driven growth framework for launching and scaling a Somali food eBook business in Calgary.

## 1) Foundation Layer (Brand Infrastructure)
Build the business like a real digital company with connected systems.

**Platforms**
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube + YouTube Shorts
- Website (Shopify or WordPress)
- Amazon Kindle (eBook distribution)
- Email list
- SMS list

All platforms should be connected into one centralized operating system.

## 2) Content Engine (Traffic Machine)
Every content asset should move people from **attention → lead → sale**.

### Instagram
**Content pillars**
- Somali food recipes
- Book highlights
- Behind-the-scenes cooking
- Cultural storytelling
- Quick cooking tips

**Posting cadence**
- 1 Reel daily
- 1 carousel every 2 days
- Stories daily

### TikTok
TikTok is the top-of-funnel growth accelerator.

**Content types**
- 15–30 second cooking clips
- Recipe reveals
- “How Somali food is made” education videos
- Book recipe previews
- Viral hook formats

**Posting cadence**
- 1–3 videos daily

### YouTube
Use two formats:
- **YouTube Shorts:** repurpose TikTok/IG short videos to expand reach.
- **Long-form channel:** cooking episodes, culture stories, and book promotion.

## 3) Funnel System (Lead Machine)
Route all traffic into lead capture and conversion funnels.

**Core flow**
1. Social media content
2. Link in bio
3. Landing page
4. Email capture
5. Offer sequence

**Example funnel**
- Offer a free Somali recipe PDF
- Capture email signup
- Nurture and sell the eBook

## 4) Sales System
The monetization layer.

### Website Sales
**Advantages**
- Higher profit margins
- Direct customer ownership (email + SMS capture)
- Bundle opportunities

### Amazon Kindle Sales
**Advantages**
- Built-in marketplace traffic
- Platform trust/credibility
- Review engine for social proof

Run both channels in parallel for maximum reach and revenue.

## 5) Automation System
This is the high-value layer that turns the project into a scalable machine.

**Automation tools**
- Zapier
- Make.com
- Klaviyo or Mailchimp
- Notion CRM

**Automations**
- **Lead automation:** welcome sequence, recipe delivery, and book promotion after signup.
- **Customer automation:** post-purchase thank-you, review request, and upsell path.
- **Content automation:** repurpose one short video across TikTok → Instagram Reels → YouTube Shorts.

## 6) Business Organization System
Centralized business operations via Notion dashboard.

**Dashboard sections**
- Content calendar
- Sales tracker
- Customer list
- Affiliate partners
- Marketing ideas backlog
- Revenue dashboard

This creates professional visibility and operating discipline.

## 7) Growth Strategy (Long Term)
Once the system is stable, scale with partnerships and paid growth.

**Scale initiatives**
- Cooking show series
- Collaborations with Somali creators
- Calgary restaurant partnerships
- Influencer campaigns
- Paid ads

Long-term objective: grow from a single eBook into a recognizable Somali food brand.

## 8) Future Revenue Streams
The eBook is phase one.

**Expansion paths**
- Physical cookbook
- Cooking classes
- YouTube monetization
- Brand partnerships
- Somali spice products
- Merchandise
